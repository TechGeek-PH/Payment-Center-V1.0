Privacy Policy

TechGeekPH Messenger Customer Support & Billing Integration

Effective date: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TechGeekPH Solutions & Services Inc. ("TechGeekPH," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when customers communicate with our Facebook Page through Messenger and when our internal systems provide customer-support, account, and billing notifications.

We use Messenger information to serve existing and prospective TechGeekPH customers, link a Messenger conversation to the correct customer account when authorized, respond to support requests, and provide relevant service or billing information.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features a customer uses and the permissions made available by Meta, we may process:

  • Messenger Page-scoped user ID (PSID) and related Messenger identifiers.
  • Messenger display name or profile information made available to our Page.
  • Messages, replies, postbacks, delivery/read events, and other interaction metadata sent to or involving our Page.
  • Page Inbox labels or label-change information used by our staff to associate a Messenger contact with a TechGeekPH account number.
  • Internal customer-account information, such as account number, service plan, billing amount, due date, payment status, and service status, when needed to provide the requested service.

2. How we use information

We process this information for legitimate business and customer-service purposes, including to:

  • identify the correct customer account connected to a Messenger conversation;
  • respond to customer questions and service requests;
  • provide account, billing, payment, reconnection, and service-status notifications;
  • prevent incorrect account linking, duplicate notifications, fraud, or misuse;
  • maintain operational records and troubleshoot our customer-support systems; and
  • comply with applicable laws, regulations, and Meta platform requirements.

3. How information is stored and processed

Our customer-support and billing workflow may use services such as Meta's Messenger Platform and Google Workspace services, including Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, to securely process authorized business records. Access is limited to personnel and service providers who need the information to operate or support these functions.

4. Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell Messenger Platform Data. We may disclose information to service providers that process data on our behalf, to Meta as required for Messenger functionality, when required by law, or when necessary to protect customers, TechGeekPH, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats, or legal claims.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, account administration, billing, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations. Messenger identifiers may be de-linked or deleted when they are no longer required, subject to legitimate retention obligations.

6. Access, correction, and data deletion

Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with this Messenger integration by emailing contactus@techgeekph.com. Please include enough information to identify the relevant TechGeekPH account or Messenger interaction. We may request reasonable verification before changing or deleting account-linked data.

Valid deletion requests will be processed subject to records we are required or permitted to retain for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect customer information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

This integration is intended for customers and authorized account contacts and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, legal requirements, or platform requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
