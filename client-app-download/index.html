Android

Official TechGeekPH Android application

TechGeekPH Client
Android APK · Version 3.0.0
Download Android App
1
Download the APKTap the Download Android App button above and wait for the APK file to finish downloading.
2
Open the downloaded fileOpen your browser Downloads or the Files app, then tap TechGeekPH-Client-v3.0.0.apk.
3
Allow installation when askedIf Android shows “Install unknown apps,” allow your browser or Files app to install this app. You can turn this permission off again after installation.
4
Install and openTap Install, then Open. The TechGeekPH Client icon will appear with your other applications.
Already installed? You do not need to reinstall just to use the Client Portal. Open your existing TechGeekPH Client app normally.

iPhone / iPad

Add the Client Portal to your Home Screen

TechGeekPH Client Portal
iOS / iPadOS · Home Screen Web App
Open Client Portal in Safari
1
Open in SafariOpen the TechGeekPH Client Portal using Safari on your iPhone or iPad. If the link opened in another browser, copy it and open it in Safari.
2
Tap the Share buttonIn Safari, tap the Share icon — the square with an upward arrow. On some iPad layouts it may appear near the top of the screen.
3
Select “Add to Home Screen”Scroll through the Share menu and tap Add to Home Screen. If you see an “Open as Web App” option, keep it enabled for the app-like experience.
4
Tap AddConfirm the name “TechGeekPH Client,” then tap Add. The icon will appear on your Home Screen.
5
Open from the Home ScreenUse the new TechGeekPH icon from now on. It opens the same secure Client Portal without needing a separate App Store application.
No separate iOS App Store app yet. The Home Screen version uses the official TechGeekPH Client Portal and works like an installed web app. Do not install unofficial TechGeekPH apps from other sources.

You can always use the Client Portal directly from any supported browser.
